How these numbers are calculated
Effective gross income
Gross annual rent minus vacancy and credit loss.
Net operating income (NOI)
Effective gross income minus operating expenses such as property taxes, insurance, maintenance, management, and utilities you pay. It excludes mortgage payments, depreciation, income taxes, and capital improvements.
Cap rate
NOI divided by the price. Value at a market cap rate is NOI divided by that rate.
Gross rent multiplier
Price divided by gross annual rent.
Debt service coverage (DSCR)
NOI divided by the annual loan payment. Below 1.0, income does not cover the payment.
Cash flow after debt
NOI minus the annual loan payment (standard amortizing loan).
Cash-on-cash return
Cash flow after debt divided by cash invested (down payment plus closing costs).
Break-even occupancy
The share of full rent needed to cover operating expenses and the loan payment: (operating expenses + debt service) ÷ gross annual rent.
