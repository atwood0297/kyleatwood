Your Options

Two Ways to Sell.
Compare Both.

Option 1

A Cash Offer from Kyle

Kyle acting as the buyer
  • Purchased as-is, with no repairs or prep on your end
  • No showings, and you help choose the closing date
  • A simpler process with fewer moving parts
  • Typically below full market value, because the buyer takes on the repair cost and risk
  • Only available if the property fits what Kyle is looking for
Option 2

List With Kyle

Kyle acting as your agent
  • Exposure to the full market, usually the best path to top price
  • Professional pricing, marketing, and negotiation on your side
  • Represented by a licensed agent through ERA Martin & Associates
  • Takes longer, and involves showings and buyer inspections
  • Repairs and prep may affect your final price

You are never required to choose either one. If Kyle is the buyer, he is not your agent. See the disclosure below.

Questions

Straight Answers.

Does submitting an inquiry guarantee an offer or a sale?

No. An inquiry is simply a way to start a conversation. It does not guarantee that Kyle will make an offer, and it does not obligate you to sell. Not every property fits what Kyle is looking for.

Will you actually buy my house, or just pass it to someone else?

Kyle buys properties to renovate and resell himself. He does not wholesale or assign purchase contracts to other buyers.

Will a cash offer be lower than listing my home?

Often, yes. A buyer who purchases as-is takes on the repair cost and the risk, so the price is usually below what a fully prepared home could bring on the open market. That is why Kyle will walk you through both numbers so you can decide which matters more to you: price or simplicity.

You are also a licensed agent. Isn't that a conflict of interest?

It is a fair question, and it is the reason for the disclosure on this page. When Kyle buys for his own account he is the buyer, not your agent, and his interests are different from yours. You are free to get other offers and to talk to an attorney or another real estate professional before you decide.

Do I have to pay anything to submit an inquiry or get an offer?

No. There is no fee to submit an inquiry or to receive an opinion of value. The terms of any purchase, including who pays which closing costs, are negotiated and written into the purchase contract.

What areas do you buy in?

Chillicothe and Ross County, Ohio, and the immediately surrounding area. Kyle is actively looking for his next renovation project locally.

How fast can a sale close?

It depends on the property, the title, and the terms you want. Kyle will give you a realistic timeline up front instead of a headline promise.
